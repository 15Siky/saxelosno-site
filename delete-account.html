Delete your account

In the app (easiest)

  1. Open Saxelosno and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (⚙️).
  3. Tap Delete account (ანგარიშის წაშლა) and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email saxelosnoapp@gmail.com with the phone number on your account. We may send an SMS code to that number to confirm it's you. We will delete the account within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, phone number and email;
  • Mechanic profile, photos, working hours and statistics;
  • Chats, messages and chat photos;
  • Your reviews, block list and notification tokens.

What is kept

  • Reports you sent, for safety, no longer linked to you;
  • Backups, fully cleared within 30 days;
  • Subscription history at Google Play or the App Store, which they manage. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play or App Store settings.
